About Us

A Shardlow upholstery workroom built around care, comfort, and craft.

Bridgecraft is a family run upholstery and furniture workroom in Derbyshire. The team restores existing pieces and creates handmade furniture for homes, designers, and commercial clients.

The philosophy

Beautiful pieces you will love for years to come.

Every piece of Bridgecraft furniture is handmade with careful consideration of the finest details, from the design concept through to the finished article. The work balances contemporary design with a classic twist, giving your furniture optimum beauty and comfort for your home.

If you are looking for a new suite, the one you already have could be the one you want. Re-upholstery can transform a much-loved piece at a fraction of the cost of buying new, while keeping the shape, scale, and comfort that already works in your room.

Custom design service

Made to order, adapted to your needs.

Bridgecraft furniture is handmade to order, so customising a design to suit your exact needs is simple. The workroom can also make pieces designed specifically for your project, with experienced input to ensure the design is practical, comfortable, and built well.

What we do

Upholstery services for old favourites and new commissions.

Assess

Talk through your piece, fabric ideas, comfort needs, measurements, and how the furniture will be used.

Advise

Get practical guidance on foam, feathers, loose covers, repairs, frame suitability, and finish.

Craft

Work is carried out with attention to structure, stitching, tension, fabric matching, and final presentation.

Install

Domestic pieces and larger commercial seating projects are finished with the same care for detail.
